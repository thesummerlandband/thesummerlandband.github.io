The band

A musical tour of Americana, with Brazilian and French songs as an added treat for the ear.

Gaby Mandelik's powerhouse vocals front the band, with Bruce Bigenho on keys, Greg Leroy on guitar and vocals, and Tom Henderson on harmonica. Their résumés include sideman work with Big Mama Thornton and Jimmy Reed, records with Crazy Horse, international tours and decades of Santa Barbara gigs.

Gaby Mandelik

Lead vocals, percussion

Gaby began her career as an actress and appears in numerous TV movies. She sang and played percussion with The Henderson Bros. Band and has performed at SOhO many times with the SBCC Kind of Blue jazz combo.

Bruce Bigenho

Keys

A jazz pianist and part of the Santa Barbara music scene for the better part of 40 years, Bruce has performed in numerous ensembles, toured internationally and appears on many CDs. His love of Latin music has led to recordings with many salsa groups in Los Angeles.

Greg Leroy

Guitar, vocals

Greg's credits run from sideman work with blues greats including George Smith, Big Mama Thornton, Pee Wee Crayton, Big Joe Turner and Jimmy Reed to major record deals as a member of Crazy Horse. He lives in Santa Barbara, where he performs and teaches.

Tom Henderson

Harmonica

Tom has been part of the Santa Barbara music scene for 50 years, starting with well-remembered bands such as The News, Bando and Moment's Notice, then performing for more than two decades with his brother as The Henderson Brothers. He plays jazz chromatic and blues harmonica, and is willing and able to perform in a tutu.
